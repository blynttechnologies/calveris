How to Preview & Deploy

  1. Open the live dev server at http://localhost:3000/
  2. Click 5 Redesigns (PDF) in the top navbar or open the floating Redesign Studio in the bottom right corner.
  3. Click any theme to watch the entire 7-page site transform instantly in real-time!
  4. Tell Antigravity which design you want to lock in as your permanent default, or customize further!

Need Custom Adjustments?

Every concept is 100% modular. You can combine elements across concepts (e.g. the midnight background of Mayfair Heritage with the structured data tables of City Executive, or the terracotta warmth of Nordic Atelier with the Swiss grid). Simply reply in chat with your preference.
